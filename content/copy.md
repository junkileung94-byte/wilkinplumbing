## SERVICES

**Plumbing Services offered**

- **Residential Plumbing** — From leaky faucets to clogged drains and pipes, I can handle any residential plumbing issue. I offer a fast and reliable service that you can count on.
- **Commercial Plumbing** — I understand that plumbing issues can disrupt your business. I try to work around your business operation to give the best service for commercial properties.
- **Backflow Prevention** — I work with the local municipality to make sure your cross connection testing and surveys are up to date and compliant for your business. I repair, install and test all types of back flow preventer.
- **Water Heater Repair** — If your water heater is not working properly, I can diagnose and repair the issue quickly. I repair and install all types of water heaters.
- **Sewer Line Services** — I have the expertise to handle any sewer line issue, from clogs and backups to tree root infiltration.
- **Plumbing Home Inspection** — Just purchased a new home or worried about your existing. I offer a full visual plumbing inspection of your home to point out all potential faults and make a plan to fix them.
- **Bathroom Renovation** — I offer full bathroom renovation services including tile, electrical and all general building work. Start to finish I take care of the entire project.
- **Plumbing Maintenance** — Regular plumbing maintenance can help prevent costly repairs down the road. I can help you keep your plumbing system in top condition with my maintenance services.

## FAQ (verbatim)

**What types of plumbing fixtures can you install?**
I can install a wide variety of plumbing fixtures, including sinks, toilets, showers, bathtubs, and much more. While I typically supply and install the fixtures myself, I'm also happy to work with customer-supplied items. Essentially, if it's plumbing-related, I can install it.

**How much will it cost me?**
Each call can vary depending on the issue and how complex it is to fix. My pricing is based on time and materials. My hourly rate is $140 + tax, with the first hour at your property billed as a minimum charge and materials charged separately if needed. Some services and supplied items, like toilets and faucets, have set prices, but these are all based on the time and materials required to get the job done right.

**Do you charge for estimates?**
Most of my estimates are free, and I can often provide a quote just from photos you send me. For bigger projects, I'm happy to do a site visit at no cost, though these are usually scheduled at specific times during the week, so you might need to be flexible. If you need me to come at a time that's more convenient for you, I can do that too, but a small fee would apply.

**How many plumbers are on staff?**
Wilkin Plumbing is currently run solely by me, Roy. I'm planning to take on an apprentice in the future, but for now, I handle all the work myself. For larger jobs, I occasionally bring in a few trusted journeyman plumbers, but they always work alongside me—I'm on-site to make sure every job meets my high standards.
