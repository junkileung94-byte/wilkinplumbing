On the ground

What plumbing in Clearview actually involves

Clearview owns and operates six municipal residential water systems — Stayner, Creemore, New Lowell, Nottawa (McKean), Colling-Woodlands and Buckingham Woods — and every one of them is fed by groundwater wells rather than surface water. Wastewater is treated at the Stayner plant, which discharges to Lamont Creek, and at Creemore's newer membrane plant, which outfalls to the Mad River.

Outside those six systems the township is well-and-septic country. Groundwater supply across the whole township means hardness is a constant, whether you are on a municipal well or your own.
